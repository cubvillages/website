Privacy policy

The Council for the Unrecognized Bedouin Villages ("the Council") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect on negevvillages.org and what we do with it.

Who is responsible for your information

The Council for the Unrecognized Bedouin Villages (public benefit company no. 513813584), Abu Qrinat (tribe), postal code 84917, is responsible for the information collected on this site. For any privacy question or request: media@unrecognizedvillages.org

What we collect

  • Your email address, only if you choose to sign up for the newsletter. You are not legally required to provide it, but without it we cannot send you updates.
  • Details you send us when you contact us by WhatsApp, phone or email.
  • Basic technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, recorded automatically by the site's service providers. The site has no tracking, analytics or advertising tools.

What we do with it

  • Send you the newsletter: updates on the Council's work and on the villages.
  • Reply to your messages.

We keep your email address for as long as you are subscribed to the newsletter. If you unsubscribe, we keep only a note that you asked not to receive emails from us, so that we can honor your request. Other messages are kept only as long as we need them to handle your request.

We do not sell, rent or pass on your details to others for marketing.

Who else receives it

  • Mailchimp (Intuit, United States) manages our mailing list. Your email address is stored on its servers outside Israel. Mailchimp also records whether you opened the newsletter and which links you clicked.
  • GitHub hosts the site and Google Fonts provides the fonts. Both receive your IP address when you browse the site.
  • YouTube (Google) shows the video on the home page. We use the version that sets no cookies until you press play. After that, YouTube's privacy rules apply.
  • Authorities, only if the law requires us.

Your rights

  • Unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time, using the link at the bottom of every email.
  • Ask to see, correct or delete your information.

For any request or question: media@unrecognizedvillages.org

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its date.
