Accessibility statement

The Council for the Unrecognized Bedouin Villages wants everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to browse and use this website comfortably and independently. We are working to bring the site in line with WCAG 2.1 level AA and with Israel's Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 2013.

What the site offers

  • The site is available in Hebrew, Arabic and English, with the correct reading direction for each language.
  • Images have alternative text descriptions.
  • The site is built to work on small screens and mobile devices.

Known limitations

Some parts of the site may not yet be fully accessible. Third-party content, such as the New Israel Fund donation form, the newsletter signup form and embedded videos, is run by its providers, and its accessibility is determined by them.

Ran into a problem?

If you could not use part of the site, or you have a suggestion, write to us and we will look into it promptly: media@unrecognizedvillages.org. Please include the page address and a description of the problem, and if possible your browser and any assistive technology you use.
